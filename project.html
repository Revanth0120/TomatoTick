The technique

Four moves, endlessly repeatable

Short sprints beat long slogs. The Pomodoro cycle keeps your brain fresh and your streak alive.

1

Pick one task

Write a single goal for the sprint. Specific beats ambitious.

2

Focus for 25

Phone away, tab quiet. The ring drains while you go deep.

3

Break for 5

Stand up, stretch, sip water. A bell tells you when to return.

4

Long rest after 4

Every fourth tomato earns a 15-minute reset. Then go again.
